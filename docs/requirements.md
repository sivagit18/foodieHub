# FoodieHub – Multi-Restaurant Food Ordering Application

## 1. Project Overview

FoodieHub is a full-stack, multi-restaurant food ordering application that allows customers to explore food items from multiple registered restaurants, browse restaurant menus, add food items to their cart, and place orders online.

The platform allows restaurant owners to register independently, manage their restaurant profiles, upload food items, manage menus, and receive customer orders.

## 2. User Roles

### 2.1 Customer
- Register and Login.
- Browse all available food items.
- Search for food.
- Explore food categories.
- View registered restaurants.
- Browse individual restaurant menus.
- Add food items to cart.
- Place orders.
- Make online payments.
- Track order status.
- View order history.
- Submit ratings and reviews.

### 2.2 Restaurant Owner
- Register restaurant.
- Login to restaurant dashboard.
- Manage restaurant profile.
- Add food categories.
- Add, edit and delete food items.
- Upload food images.
- Manage food availability.
- Receive customer orders.
- Accept or reject orders.
- Update order status.
- View sales reports.

### 2.3 Admin
- Manage customers.
- Manage restaurant registrations.
- Approve or reject restaurants.
- Manage food categories.
- Monitor food items.
- Monitor customer orders.
- Manage platform operations.
- View reports and statistics.

## 5. Business Rules

1. Multiple restaurants can register on the platform.
2. Each restaurant has its own independent dashboard.
3. Each food item must belong to a restaurant.
4. Customers can view food items from multiple restaurants on the homepage.
5. Customers can access individual restaurant menus.
6. Restaurant owners can manage only their own restaurant information and food items.
7. The initial cart implementation will support one restaurant per order.
8. Every successful order must generate a unique order ID.
9. Restaurant owners must be able to update order status.
10. Only approved restaurants will be visible to customers.
